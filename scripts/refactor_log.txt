Category 1: Duplicated validation
-----------------------------------
Reviewed: Account._validate_type() (internal, raises ValueError) vs.
validate_account_type() in dashboard/validators.py (boundary-level,
raises ValidationError). These check the same VALID_TYPES membership
but serve different purposes — one is a defensive backstop against
direct misuse of the Account constructor, the other is the CLI/file
boundary check. This is intentional layered validation, not
accidental duplication (documented in Lesson 19). No change made.
